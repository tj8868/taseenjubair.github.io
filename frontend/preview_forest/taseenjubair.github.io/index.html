Public Health

My research focuses on population health, environmental epidemiology, and disease surveillance in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs), aligning directly with Global Burden of Disease (GBD) priorities to mitigate preventable mortality and morbidity. Grounded in applied biostatistics, environmental risk modeling, and health systems research, my work investigates early cardiac event monitoring and chronic disease detection, toxicological and atmospheric pollution exposure assessments (including heavy-metal carcinogenic risks and satellite-derived urban formaldehyde anomalies), and the structural and operational vulnerability of frontline healthcare facilities in disaster-prone coastal corridors. By bridging complex field epidemiology, spatial analytics, and rigorous evidence synthesis, my objective is to generate actionable, policy-relevant evidence that strengthens disease prevention, environmental regulation, and health infrastructure resilience.

Technical & Engineering Toolkit

Expertise spans statistical modeling and data science (Python, R, SQL, mixed-effects models, repeated-measures ANOVA, clustering), geospatial analysis and GeoAI (ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Google Earth Engine), automated pipeline engineering, and rigorous evidence synthesis under PRISMA-ScR and JBI methodologies.
